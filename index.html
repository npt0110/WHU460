    Technical specification

    Taken from the PVD HWU short specification. Values are kept in the manufacturer's original units.

    Geometry follows the dimensions stated on drawing S00012731 sheets 1–4 (skid length 16,225, rail spacing 15,240, width 3,500, and elevations +2,874 / +3,734 / +8,770 / +11,650 / +14,090 / +16,500 / +36,617). Dimensions not stated on the drawing were scaled from the views and rounded. The wellhead is hypothetical, as noted on the drawing. Positions of the rotary table, manifolds and escape lines are estimates. Auxiliary equipment (power pack, generator, compressor, MCC, accumulator, mud pumps and tanks, diesel tank, pipe racks) is sized and specified from the short spec and the HWU brochure, and laid out on the required 35 × 40 m platform by estimate, since no rig layout drawing was supplied. The model is for presentation and training, not for fabrication.
